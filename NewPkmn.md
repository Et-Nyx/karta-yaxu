# Generation 1 Pokémon: Alternate Forms & Next-Gen Evolutions

This list covers all the additions given to the original 151 Pokémon (from Bulbasaur to Mew) in later generations, including new evolutions, pre-evolutions (babies), Mega Evolutions, Gigantamax forms, Regional forms, Convergent species, and Paradox forms.

---

## 1. Next-Generation Evolutions & Pre-Evolutions

These are the Pokémon that were added to a Gen 1 Pokémon's standard evolutionary family in later generations (Generations 2 through 9).

### Pre-evolutions (Baby Pokémon):
*   **Pichu (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Pikachu
*   **Cleffa (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Clefairy
*   **Igglybuff (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Jigglypuff
*   **Tyrogue (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan, or Hitmontop
*   **Smoochum (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Jynx
*   **Elekid (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Electabuzz
*   **Magby (Gen 2)** -> Evolves into Magmar
*   **Munchlax (Gen 4)** -> Evolves into Snorlax
*   **Happiny (Gen 4)** -> Evolves into Chansey
*   **Mime Jr. (Gen 4)** -> Evolves into Mr. Mime

### Cross-Generation Evolutions:
*   **Zubat / Golbat** -> Crobat (Gen 2)
*   **Oddish / Gloom** -> Bellossom (Gen 2 - Split Evolution)
*   **Poliwag / Poliwhirl** -> Politoed (Gen 2 - Split Evolution)
*   **Slowpoke** -> Slowking (Gen 2 - Split Evolution)
*   **Onix** -> Steelix (Gen 2)
*   **Horsea / Seadra** -> Kingdra (Gen 2)
*   **Scyther** -> Scizor (Gen 2) & Kleavor (Gen 8 - Hisuian Split Evolution)
*   **Eevee** -> Espeon & Umbreon (Gen 2), Leafeon & Glaceon (Gen 4), Sylveon (Gen 6)
*   **Porygon** -> Porygon2 (Gen 2) -> Porygon-Z (Gen 4)
*   **Chansey** -> Blissey (Gen 2)
*   **Hitmonlee / Hitmonchan** -> Connect via Tyrogue to Hitmontop (Gen 2 - Branch Evolution)
*   **Magnemite / Magneton** -> Magnezone (Gen 4)
*   **Lickitung** -> Lickilicky (Gen 4)
*   **Rhyhorn / Rhydon** -> Rhyperior (Gen 4)
*   **Tangela** -> Tangrowth (Gen 4)
*   **Electabuzz** -> Electivire (Gen 4)
*   **Magmar** -> Magmortar (Gen 4)
*   **Mankey / Primeape** -> Annihilape (Gen 9)

## 2. Mega Evolutions (Gen 6 & Legends: Z-A)

Mega Evolution allows fully evolved Pokémon to undergo a temporary transformation in battle. While the mechanic debuted in Generation 6, it returned in Pokémon Legends: Z-A with brand-new forms for classic Kanto Pokémon.

### Original Mega Evolutions (Gen 6):
*   Mega Venusaur
*   Mega Charizard X & Mega Charizard Y
*   Mega Blastoise
*   Mega Beedrill
*   Mega Pidgeot
*   Mega Alakazam
*   Mega Slowbro
*   Mega Gengar
*   Mega Kangaskhan
*   Mega Pinsir
*   Mega Gyarados
*   Mega Aerodactyl
*   Mega Mewtwo X & Mega Mewtwo Y

### New Mega Evolutions (Pokémon Legends: Z-A):
*   **Mega Raichu X & Mega Raichu Y** (Introduced in the Mega Dimension DLC)
*   Mega Clefable
*   Mega Victreebel
*   Mega Starmie
*   Mega Dragonite

## 3. Gigantamax Forms (Gen 8)

A special variation of Dynamaxing in the Galar region that changes the Pokémon's size and design while granting an exclusive G-Max Move.

*   Gigantamax Venusaur
*   Gigantamax Charizard
*   Gigantamax Blastoise
*   Gigantamax Butterfree
*   Gigantamax Pikachu

*   Gigantamax Kingler
*   Gigantamax Lapras
*   Gigantamax Eevee
*   Gigantamax Snorlax

## 4. Regional Forms

Species that adapted uniquely to specific regions, altering their typing, appearance, and abilities.

### Alolan Forms (Gen 7 - Hawaii-inspired):
*   Alolan Rattata & Alolan Raticate
*   Alolan Raichu (Evolves from standard Pikachu in Alola)
*   Alolan Sandshrew & Alolan Sandslash
*   Alolan Vulpix & Alolan Ninetales
*   Alolan Diglett & Alolan Dugtrio
*   Alolan Meowth & Alolan Persian
*   Alolan Geodude, Alolan Graveler, & Alolan Golem
*   Alolan Grimer & Alolan Muk
*   Alolan Exeggutor (Evolves from standard Exeggcute in Alola)
*   Alolan Marowak (Evolves from standard Cubone in Alola)

### Galarian Forms (Gen 8 - UK-inspired):
*   Galarian Meowth
*   Galarian Ponyta & Galarian Rapidash
*   Galarian Slowpoke & Galarian Slowbro (Also evolves into Galarian Slowking, modifying a Gen 2 evolution!)
*   Galarian Farfetch'd
*   Galarian Weezing (Evolves from standard Koffing in Galar)
*   Galarian Mr. Mime
*   Galarian Articuno
*   Galarian Zapdos
*   Galarian Moltres

### Hisuian Forms (Gen 8 - Ancient Sinnoh-inspired):
*   Hisuian Growlithe & Hisuian Arcanine
*   Hisuian Voltorb & Hisuian Electrode

### Paldean Forms (Gen 9 - Spain-inspired):
*   **Paldean Tauros** (Has three distinct forms: Combat Breed, Blaze Breed, and Aqua Breed)

---

## 5. Regional Evolutions

Unlike standard Cross-Generation evolutions, these are entirely brand-new species that only evolve from the Regional Forms of Gen 1 Pokémon.

*   **Galarian Meowth** -> Perrserker (Gen 8)
*   **Galarian Farfetch'd** -> Sirfetch'd (Gen 8)
*   **Galarian Mr. Mime** -> Mr. Rime (Gen 8)

---

## 6. Convergent Evolutions (Gen 9)

These are completely different species of Pokémon that look almost identical to Gen 1 Pokémon due to convergent evolution (adapting to similar ecological niches), but are entirely unrelated biologically.

*   **Toedscool & Toedscruel** (Convergent forms of Tentacool & Tentacruel)
*   **Wiglett & Wugtrio** (Convergent forms of Diglett & Dugtrio)

---

## 7. Paradox Forms (Bonus - Gen 9)

Paradox Pokémon are mysterious, ancient past or distant future versions of modern Pokémon brought into the Paldea region.

*   **Scream Tail** (Ancient relative of Jigglypuff)
*   **Sandy Shocks** (Ancient relative of Magneton)
